# Maid Builder 1.2.0 — Service Buildings

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.219 or newer, Touhou Little Maid 1.5.3 or newer

Upgrading from 1.1.0: your worlds, territories and running build jobs carry over.

## New

- **5 service buildings** (new "Service" tab in the template catalog). They work while they are intact; each effect can be switched off or tuned in the server config.
  - **Trading Post** (level 3): every 30 minutes a wandering trader comes with two llamas and stays for about 20 minutes.
  - **Travel between trading posts:** use the cartography table on the trading post's counter and pick another of your trading posts to go there (across territories and dimensions; one emerald per trip, free in creative mode). Sneak to use the table as usual.
  - **Village Hall** (level 2): every 20 minutes a villager moves in, until three live around it.
  - **Watchtower** (level 3): hostile mobs within 48 blocks glow, and you are told when a raid starts nearby.
  - **Canteen** (level 2): your maids heal inside the territory; twice as fast together with the level 2 healing bonus.
  - **Shrine** (level 4): your maids in the territory gain favorability once a day.
- Level 5 now also asks for 2 kinds of service buildings.

## Improved

- **Maids find the way round to material containers:** when the chest is on the other side of a building and the way round is long, a maid no longer stands at the wall; she finds the way herself. Taking down scaffolding uses the same fallback.
- A maid that starts fetching mid-jump (or while being pushed) no longer waits 10 seconds for nothing.
- Lanterns, torches and the like wait for their support to be built instead of being left for the player too soon.
- Maids no longer climb the job's scaffolding inside a building as if it were a ladder (which could keep one going up and down inside a tall tower).
