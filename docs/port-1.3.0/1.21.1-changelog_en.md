# Maid Builder 1.3.0 — New Look, Faster Builders

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.219 or newer, Touhou Little Maid 1.5.3 or newer

Upgrading from 1.2.0: your worlds, territories and running build jobs carry over.

## New

- **Redesigned screens** in a dark blueprint style:
  - **Territory screen:** tabs on the left; the overview shows what the next level still needs; build jobs are cards with a progress bar and a clear "N blocks need you" button; maid cards show their backpack.
  - **Template catalog:** categories, list and a detail pane side by side. Click to look at a building (locked ones too), double-click or press the button to place it.
  - **Material list:** progress bar, filters with counts (missing / short / enough / all) and a bar per row showing where the materials are.
  - **Wand and build mode overlays:** compact cards with progress, missing materials as item icons and key hints.
- **Blocks left for you stay visible:** a job with blocks the maids could not place stays in the queue until they are dealt with. Click it to see every block with the reason, outline them in the world, hand them back to the maids or skip them.

## Improved

- **Building on scaffolding is much faster** (towers 20–35% faster in our tests):
  - On top of scaffolding a maid places a block as soon as she picks it.
  - Scaffolding goes up where it serves the most blocks, so she climbs down less often.
  - Moving along the top she finishes what is near the column on both sides before going further out.
  - Before climbing she stocks up at the material container if she is short for the blocks ahead.
- **No more blocks hung in mid-air ahead of time:** a maid who lacks the materials for the layer in turn no longer places a crafting table or stonecutter where the upper floor will be; she fetches materials first.
- **Materials are taken in the order they are needed.** With a full inventory a maid puts back what she needs last instead of standing at the chest.
- Maids walk over carpet and thin snow when finding the way round to a container.

## Fixed

- A maid could stand still inside a building whose doorstep is a stair or slab (canteen, warehouse, dormitory, village hall).
- Scaffolding now respects protection mods, both when it is put up and when it is taken down.
- Changing the clearing mode of a job with blocks left for the player could make the job fail to save.
- A tiny malformed upload could make the server reserve a large amount of memory. Uploads are now checked off the server thread.
- A maid who had just fetched materials could give up her walk to the scaffolding.
- A maid could go up and down the same scaffolding without placing anything.
