# Maid Builder 1.1.0 — Territories

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.219 or newer, Touhou Little Maid 1.5.3 or newer

Upgrading from 1.0.0: your worlds and running build jobs carry over (existing jobs don't break any blocks; see "Fixing wrong blocks" below).

## New

- **Territories:**
  - **Territory Flag** (crafting table: gold ingot, any banner, gold ingot on top; one stick in the middle of each of the two rows below): the area around it becomes your territory, 48 blocks in radius at level 1 and growing as it levels up. Up to 10 territories per player, with flags at least 10 chunks apart. Hold the flag or press `G` to see the border.
  - **Build mode:** press `Caps Lock` inside your territory, pick a building from the template catalog, place it where you look, and it joins the build queue. Builder maids work through the queue in order.
  - **11 built-in buildings:** warehouse, sugar cane farm, small greenhouse, lumberyard, ranch, apiary, dormitory, large greenhouse, large ranch, fishing dock and pavilion. They use only basic materials such as wood, cobblestone, glass and dirt.
  - **Prosperity and levels:** finished buildings and maids living in the territory raise its prosperity; once the conditions are met, hand in items at the flag to level up and unlock more templates.
  - **Territory bonuses:** maids slowly heal, greenhouse crops grow faster, ranch animals breed again in half the time, and no hostile mobs spawn. Each can be turned off in the server settings.
  - **Workplaces:** assign maids to work at a greenhouse, ranch, sugar cane farm and more. When their backpack is full they take a break; "To warehouse" sends their products to the warehouse, and they go back to work once there is room.
  - **Building integrity:** a building that loses more than 20% of its blocks stops counting until repaired; one click queues the repair.
  - **Territory material chests:** bind them once and every job in the territory uses them.
  - Levels, templates and bonuses come from data packs, so modpacks can change or add them.
- **Fixing wrong blocks:** if a different block is where the blueprint wants one, the maid breaks it and places the right one; what she breaks goes into her inventory.
  - Optionally she also clears the blueprint's air cells (only on the same layer, within 5 blocks of the building). The preview outlines in orange what would be cleared.
  - Containers, bedrock and very hard blocks are left alone. Breaking fires the normal player break event, so protection mods can stop it.
  - New command `/maidbuilder job clearing <id> [off|replace|all]`.

## Improved

- **Scaffolding extends sideways:** up on her scaffolding, a maid now hangs scaffolding out to the side (up to 6 blocks) and walks over to build blocks at the same height, instead of climbing down to put up another column each time. Hanging scaffolding is taken down with the rest when the job is done. On wide buildings a single maid is about 14% faster, climbs down 6 times instead of 19, and needs less scaffolding.
- **Placing water no longer uses up the bucket:** the maid keeps the empty bucket.

## Changed

- Farmland and dirt paths now need dirt (they used to ask for farmland items you can't get in survival).
- Territory templates always place their water, whatever the "Build fluids" server setting says.
- New server settings: clearing mode, clearing radius, maximum hardness to break, and territory settings (territories per player, flag spacing, bonus switches and values, and more).
